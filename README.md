# ServicoFlash

> Open-source starter kit for local service businesses to turn customer requests into fast, organized appointments.

ServicoFlash is an early-stage open-source project for service providers such as locksmiths, electricians, plumbers, beauty professionals, and repair businesses.

The goal is simple: help small local businesses receive requests, respond through WhatsApp, and organize appointments without complex tools.

## Status

🚧 **Early development**

The repository is being prepared as the public foundation of ServicoFlash. The first release will focus on a practical appointment flow for local services.

## Planned MVP

- Service catalog with clear pricing or quote requests
- One-tap WhatsApp contact
- Appointment request form
- Business hours and service area
- Google Maps location
- Mobile-first public page
- Simple admin view for incoming requests

## Who it is for

- Locksmiths
- Electricians
- Plumbers
- Technicians
- Beauty professionals
- Independent local service providers

## Vision

Local businesses should not need expensive software or technical knowledge to have a professional online presence and respond quickly to customers.

ServicoFlash aims to provide a lightweight, customizable, and open-source foundation for that workflow.
